**Figure [n]. Within-patient change in the AI-ECG score before left ventricular systolic dysfunction, atrial fibrillation and limb fracture.**

(A) Change in the EchoNext-Mini LVEF ≤45% output, on the logit scale, in each time bin before the event. Each change is relative to the same patients' ECGs recorded 60 to 36 months before the event. The estimates come from a patient fixed-effects model, so stable differences between patients do not contribute. Vertical lines are 95% confidence intervals with patient-clustered standard errors. Markers are offset horizontally so that they do not overlap.

(B) Average pre-event slope in each group, and the differences between groups. Horizontal lines are 95% confidence intervals. The shaded band is the pre-specified equivalence margin (±Δ). The words on the right give the pre-specified reading of each difference.

AF, atrial fibrillation; AI-ECG, artificial intelligence–enabled electrocardiography; ECG, electrocardiogram; LVEF, left ventricular ejection fraction; LVSD, left ventricular systolic dysfunction.
