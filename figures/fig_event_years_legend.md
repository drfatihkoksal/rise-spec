**Figure [n]. Within-patient change in two AI-ECG models in the years before left ventricular systolic dysfunction, atrial fibrillation, limb fracture or a random date without any event.**

Each point is the change in the same patients' ECGs relative to their ECGs recorded 5 to 3 years before the event (plotted at −4 years). The estimates for the event groups come from the pre-specified patient fixed-effects event studies, and those for the no-event group from the same model fitted to that group alone, so stable differences between patients do not contribute. Vertical lines are 95% confidence intervals with patient-clustered standard errors. Points are offset horizontally so that they do not overlap. (A, B) EchoNext-Mini, change in its LVEF ≤45% output on the logit scale; up is worse. (C, D) DeepECG-SL, change in predicted LVEF in percentage points; down is worse. (A, C) MIMIC-IV, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center; (B, D) HEEDB, Massachusetts General Hospital and Emory. The no-event group had no LVSD, AF, fracture, heart failure or cardiomyopathy code at any time and was anchored at a random date within its ECG follow-up.

AF, atrial fibrillation; AI-ECG, artificial intelligence–enabled electrocardiography; ECG, electrocardiogram; LVEF, left ventricular ejection fraction; LVSD, left ventricular systolic dysfunction.
